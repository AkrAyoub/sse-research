## Entry 002 — requirement captured: automatic valueless test-ETH funding (design and source tests only)

**User requirement:** every newcomer chooses any eligible role combination and gets enough chain-native **valueless TEST ETH** automatically for that role's *actual* on-chain economic prerequisites: Beacon deposit for each validator key (32 test ETH in this PoS design), contract-defined minimum monitor bond, gas for every role's transactions, consumer escrow and monitor/executor incentives based on the chosen lease. No real Ethereum ETH or private mainnet funds. Funds belong to new operator's LOCAL independent wallets, never a shared developer wallet. No admin signup/approval.

**Correctness boundary:** a node may join native P2P and sync with zero balance; only economic actions need money. A role choice neither signs nor spends without owner's approval nor means Beacon/contract activation succeeded. A faucet/claim must be public, objectively bounded and replaceable; no single central funding approval. A finite pool cannot promise infinite test ETH to unrestricted Sybils: document budget, funding and liveness failures; permit independently contributed pools/sponsors or alternative public funders. Do not silently mint fake balances or mark a role funded without finalized on-chain receipts.

**Historical trap:** `tools/sse_pos_live_roles.py` on prior chain31339 directly decrypts one named `faucet` keystore and funds a fixed locally managed set of seven role keys; this cannot serve a real external permissionless network. `tools/sse_pos_validator_deposit.py` verifies a 32-test-ETH deposit but hardcodes chain31339 and requires separate authorized broadcast. No public chain31340 or actual faucet deployed yet.

**Status at recording:** requirement recorded, source funding architecture to be designed and tested; no faucet, transfers, independent wallet provisioning, sponsored transactions, or Beacon deposits executed.
