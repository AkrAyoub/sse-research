## Evidence strength classification for SSE

**Implementable now without additional oracle claim:** canonical SLA preimage/hash and version, raw signed response attribution, off-chain event archive with provenance, local challenge verifier, fail-closed type and replay validation, explicit `unknown`, simulator-based counterexamples, min/max financial reference and differential testing. These provide *integrity or conditional guarantees*, not universal physical truth.

**Conditionally implementable with new trusted or cryptographic assumptions:** consumer receipt attestation (trusted signer, binding and clock), TLS provenance (notary threat model), zkVM execution correctness (proof-system soundness), storage persistence proofs (data/prover/protocol specificity), hardware attestation (vendor/platform root and measurement/nonce binding), witnessed latency (observer location and clock assumptions), cross-network availability (sampling distribution and coverage assumptions).

**Not achievable as unconditional fact under the current SSE observation model:** perfect proof of absence of a provider event from a few timeouts; guaranteed physical-world soundness when the model omits a possible execution; trusted CPU reservation from a digest alone; indefinite storage durability from one successful challenge; fair immediate resolution of every disputed payment without allocating risk or assuming an arbitrator/availability bound.

## Research gaps and explicit next literature queries

1. Fair exchange and contingent payment research (observable delivery receipts, atomicity, optimistic third-party arbitration, incomplete-evidence allocation).
2. Mechanism-design models for risk of missing receipts versus truthful monitors; distinction between probabilistic confidence and **absolute** world soundness.
3. Proof-of-retrievability security models and temporal audit soundness versus service availability SLAs.
4. Authentic timestamping, clock skew and censorship-resistance models needed for deadline-based payment.
5. Distinguish novelty of the **combination** (sound interpretation + maximal partial settlement + bounded continuation) from preexisting concepts individually.

**Scientific decision:** no novelty claim beyond a candidate hypothesis until systematic closest-mechanism examination and explicit counterexample comparison are complete.

## Additional closest-work family: fair exchange and dispute evidence (important)

- N. Asokan, V. Shoup, M. Waidner, *Asynchronous Protocols for Optimistic Fair Exchange*, IEEE S&P 1998, https://research.ibm.com/publications/asynchronous-protocols-for-optimistic-fair-exchange — optimistic fair exchange with a third party in exceptions, handling temporary communication failures to the third party. **Implication:** recovering fair completion under network uncertainty is established research and may require an explicit dispute authority/trust assumption. Our held escrow is not equivalent to their termination guarantee.
- N. Asokan, V. Shoup, M. Waidner, *Optimistic Fair Exchange of Digital Signatures*, https://research.ibm.com/publications/optimistic-fair-exchange-of-digital-signatures — fair exchange of signed items with trusted-third-party resolution on faults. **Implication:** do not call evidence-dependent bilateral settlement or exceptional arbitration novel in isolation.
- N. Asokan et al., *A framework for handling payment disputes* (USENIX EC 1998 article), https://www.usenix.org/legacy/event/ec98/full_papers/asokan/asokan_html/asokan.html — a verified signed receipt can be only a **witnessed assertion**; whether it implies the real transaction depends on the trust assumptions and semantics of the specific payment system. **Implication:** this prior work already states the core distinction between signature authenticity and physical-event truth. Cite it explicitly, rather than claiming SSE discovered this epistemic distinction.
- Juels & Kaliski, *PORs: Proofs of Retrievability for Large Files*, CCS 2007, https://doi.org/10.1145/1315245.1315317 — formal cryptographic precedent for outsourced storage retrievability. **Implication:** any proposed SSE storage proof must be compared against POR and related protocols, not solely Filecoin PoSt.

The SSE candidate contribution must be differentiated against **financially partial releases over a set of still-possible service histories**, **per-service evidence policies**, **unresolved exposure budgets**, and **continued service admission**, not fair exchange itself. This review remains selective and does not establish novelty.
